Savings Goal Calculator: How Much to Save Per Month

How it works

Given a target, a deadline and an interest rate, this works out the monthly deposit that reaches your goal, counting the growth on what you've already saved.

How to use it

  1. Enter your goal and what you've saved so far.
  2. Set the number of years and the interest rate.
  3. See the monthly amount needed.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I save each month?

Divide what you still need by the number of months, then reduce it by the interest you'll earn. This calculator does that math for you.
