Tip Calculator & Bill Splitter

How it works

Add a tip percentage to any bill and split it evenly, with an option to round each share up to the next whole amount.

How to use it

  1. Enter the bill total.
  2. Choose a tip percentage and the number of people.
  3. Each person's share appears instantly.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

In the US, 15–20% is standard for sit-down restaurant service, and 18–20% is common for good service.
