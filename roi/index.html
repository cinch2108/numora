ROI Calculator: Return on Investment, Margin & Markup

How it works

ROI = (final value − cost) ÷ cost. When you enter how long you held the investment, it also gives the annualized return (CAGR) so investments of different lengths can be compared fairly.

How to use it

  1. Enter the cost and the final value.
  2. Optionally enter how many years you held it.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is profit divided by the selling price, and markup is profit divided by cost. A 50% markup is a 33.3% margin.
